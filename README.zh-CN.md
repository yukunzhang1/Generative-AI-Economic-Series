# 生成式 AI 经济学

**资本、市场与信息**

七篇论文考察生成式 AI 如何改变智能生产的经济机制、重组市场与平台，并影响信息与激励。该系列结合相互补充的理论和计算研究，讨论价值创造、分配与治理。

[访问学术专题主页](https://yukunzhang1.github.io/research/ai-economics/) · [English](README.md) · [下载 BibTeX](CITATION.bib)

## AI 资本与基础设施

投资、使用机会与竞争如何塑造 AI 的价值？

**1. [The Economics of Digital Intelligence Capital: Endogenous Depreciation and the Structural Jevons Paradox](papers/digital-intelligence-capital.md)**

研究相对能力、反馈积累与下游智能体设计如何影响资本折旧、计算需求和市场集中度。

[论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12339) · [海报](paper1_poster.pdf) · [中英文介绍](https://yukunzhang1.github.io/research/ai-economics/digital-intelligence-capital/)

**2. [Beyond Private or Public: Large Language Models as Quasi-Public Goods in the AI Economy](papers/quasi-public-goods.md)**

将基座模型作为准公共基础设施，讨论使用规则与外部性如何影响创新激励和社会福利。

**3. [Generative AI as a Non-Convex Supply Shock: Market Bifurcation and Welfare Analysis](papers/non-convex-supply-shock.md)**

研究低生产成本与生成内容拥堵并存时的市场分化和调整过程。

[论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12488) · [海报](Poster_Generative%20AI%20as%20a%20Non-Convex%20Supply%20Shock%20Market%20Bifurcation%20and%20Welfare%20Analysis.pdf) · [中英文介绍](https://yukunzhang1.github.io/research/ai-economics/non-convex-supply-shock/)

**4. [The Impact of Generative AI on Content Platforms: A Two-Sided Market Analysis with Multi-Dimensional Quality Heterogeneity](papers/content-platforms.md)**

[论文](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13101) · [海报](The%20Impact%20of%20Generative%20AI%20on%20Content%20Platforms.pdf) · [中英文介绍](https://yukunzhang1.github.io/research/ai-economics/content-platforms/)

**6. [AI-Enabled Rent-Seeking: How Generative AI Alters Market Transparency and Efficiency](papers/ai-enabled-rent-seeking.md)**
